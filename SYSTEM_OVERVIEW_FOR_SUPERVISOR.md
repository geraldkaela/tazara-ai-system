# TAZARA AI Railway System - Overview for Management

## What This System Does

The TAZARA AI System is an intelligent railway scheduling platform that automatically plans train routes, assigns cargo, and optimizes operations between Tanzania and Zambia.

### Core Functions

**1. Automatic Train Scheduling**
- Takes customer orders for cargo transport
- Automatically assigns the best trains to each route
- Creates daily schedules showing which trains go where
- Considers train capacity, cargo weight, and delivery urgency

**2. Priority-Based Decision Making**
- Orders are ranked by importance (Emergency, Urgent, Priority, Normal, Low)
- High-priority customers get trains first
- System considers cargo type (medical supplies, minerals, etc.)
- Route distance affects priority (longer routes get preference)

**3. Real-Time Operations Management**
- Shows current train locations and schedules
- Tracks cargo delivered and profits
- Monitors system efficiency and performance
- Provides alerts for operational issues

## Key Business Benefits

## How It Works - Step by Step

### Step 1: Orders Come In
Customers request cargo transport with:
- Origin and destination stations
- Cargo type and weight
- Priority level (Emergency, Urgent, etc.)
- Required delivery dates

### Step 2: AI Analysis
The system analyzes each order and calculates:
- Priority score (0-100 points)
- Urgency based on delivery deadlines
- Route complexity and distance factors
- Customer importance weighting

### Step 3: Smart Scheduling
The AI creates optimal schedules by:
- Grouping orders by route direction
- Selecting appropriate train sizes
- Balancing daily workload
- Resolving conflicts automatically

### Step 4: Execution & Monitoring
- Dispatches trains according to schedule
- Tracks progress in real-time
- Updates customers on delivery status
- Records performance data
